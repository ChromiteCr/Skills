Pier Nine Hall九号码头展馆

A fictional three-floor venue with an open corridor (3F), a two-floor atrium (1F + 2F) and a ring gallery round the atrium void (2F). Drag to turn it, tap a token or a room, search, choose where you are and who you are going to see. Everything on the left is read from CSS tokens; the buttons only change them.

      Scripts: search.js (name / group / room search), wayfind.js (venue model, router, directions text) and venue-map.js (the canvas map). Venue data: venue.json, validated by ../engine/venue.schema.json: areas[] (the 3F west corridor, the 1F atrium floor) are open zones with no walls, a void block (the 2F atrium) is an opening to the floor below, the 2F gallery is an area with holes (a ring round that void), floors have a short name for sentences (2F) beside the heading name (Second Floor), and the Tide Radio kiosk stands on an open floor by at + floor. The lead language is <html data-evk-lead>: the map follows it. Keyboard on the map: arrows turn and tilt, + / − zoom, [ and ] step through exhibitors, 0 resets.
