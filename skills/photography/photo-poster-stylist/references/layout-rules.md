# Minimal poster layout rules

These are working defaults, not universal laws. Override them when the medium, printer, script, accessibility need, or photographer's direction requires it; record the override.

## Canvas and bleed

- Declare trim size and medium before composing.
- For print, obtain bleed from the printer. If unavailable, use no assumed bleed and mark it unresolved.
- Keep live text inside a safe area of at least 5% of the shorter trim edge unless deliberate edge tension is part of the confirmed concept.
- Build at final aspect ratio. Scaling later often changes perceived spacing and type size.

## Grid

- Start with 6 columns for portrait and 8 for landscape; use another grid when the content clearly benefits.
- Outer margin: 6–10% of the shorter edge.
- Use a base spacing unit of about 1% of the shorter edge and multiples of it.
- Align edges deliberately. Near-alignments that miss the grid usually look accidental.

## Hierarchy

- One visual mass should dominate by area, contrast, or placement.
- A second mass may counterbalance it; remaining elements support rather than compete.
- Preserve one recognition cue from the source photograph. Do not compensate for weak abstraction with many literal details.
- Negative space is an active shape. Check it at thumbnail size.

## Color

- Maximum three visible colors by default, including the background.
- Assign roles: background, foreground, accent. Do not add near-duplicate shades merely for polish.
- Check text contrast numerically: 4.5:1 for ordinary text, 3:1 for large text. Final legibility still requires visual review.
- Screen RGB/hex values do not guarantee print color. Request the printer's profile or proof when color fidelity matters.

## Typography

- Use supplied or properly licensed typefaces. A generic `sans-serif` fallback is portable but not metrically identical across systems.
- At most two sizes and two weights by default.
- Title carries hierarchy; subtitle supplies context; credit remains subordinate.
- Do not distort letterforms to fit. Shorten copy, change measure, or revise scale.

## Export review

- Inspect at thumbnail and final size.
- Confirm text is selectable in SVG unless outlines are explicitly required.
- Confirm no element unintentionally crosses trim or safe area.
- For print, verify bleed and color workflow with the actual provider rather than assuming a universal setting.
